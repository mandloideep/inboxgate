# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the InboxGate community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of personal background, identity, experience, or level of expertise.
We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Focus feedback on ideas and behavior rather than people.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's private information without permission.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, trolling, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the reporting link in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and identify the submission as a conduct report.
The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate.
Reports will be handled as privately as reasonably possible.

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
